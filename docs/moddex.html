Statement of Concern

1. Clarification on Downloads and Revenue

To remain entirely factual: Moddex.gg correctly routes file download links to official sources (such as CurseForge). Consequently, Moddex.gg does not directly steal download metrics or direct file revenue from developers.

2. The Core Problem: Reputation Damage via Unmonitored Feedback

While downloads are correctly redirected, Moddex.gg generates independent comment, rating, and feedback sections for listed mods without the authors' knowledge or consent.

Because developers are unaware these third-party comment sections exist, user bug reports and support inquiries submitted on Moddex.gg go completely unanswered. Players incorrectly assume the author is neglectful or hostile, leading to severe, unmerited reputation damage and user abandonment.

3. Platform Terms of Service & API Compliance

Moddex.gg relies on platform APIs to populate its directory. While CurseForge's Terms of Service currently permit third-party metadata retrieval, Modrinth's Terms of Service are ambiguous regarding complete catalog proxying.

We encourage community members and creators to submit formal inquiries to both CurseForge and Modrinth support and legal teams regarding potential API policy misuse at the moddex.gg domain.

Conduct Guidelines

To maintain absolute legitimacy, participants in this campaign must adhere to the following principles:

  • Remain Factual: Focus strictly on documented issues regarding unmonitored feedback sections and API compliance.
  • Zero Harassment: Do not engage in threats, personal attacks, or spam against any individuals associated with Moddex.gg. Hostility damages the validity of creator concerns.
  • Report via Official Channels: Inquire directly with CurseForge and Modrinth support regarding third-party API policies.
