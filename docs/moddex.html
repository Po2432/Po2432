Protest Moddex.gg

We are raising awareness regarding the unacceptable practices of Moddex.gg concerning mod authors and the Minecraft community.

Read the Evidence

The Concerns & Evidence

Our protest is based entirely on documented facts regarding the platform's current operating model. We are calling for transparency, proper attribution, and fundamental respect for mod creators.

📊 Fact: There are nearly 300,000 Minecraft mods currently indexed and listed on Moddex.gg.
  • Improper Attribution: Widespread instances of improper or entirely missing attribution for the original authors of the mods hosted/listed.
  • Listed Without Consent: Mods are being cataloged, listed, and rated by users on the platform without the original authors necessarily being aware their work is even on the site.
  • Ignored Community Feedback: User suggestions, bug reports, and feedback directed at mod authors are left sitting on Moddex.gg without responses. This occurs because the authors are completely unaware of the site's existence and cannot see their own users' feedback.
