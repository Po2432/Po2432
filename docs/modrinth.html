Modrinth Departure Notice

Effective Immediately: My mods will no longer be published or updated on Modrinth.

Why am I leaving Modrinth?

Due to the recent acquisition of Modrinth by Spark Universe, I have made the decision to cease all publishing on the platform. I will no longer be supporting Modrinth as a host for my projects.

Where can you find my mods now?

All active development, future updates, and new projects will be exclusively available on my CurseForge profile.

What happens to existing Modrinth projects and Modpacks?

To ensure a smooth transition for the community, my projects on Modrinth will be archived, not deleted.

  • Modpacks will not break: If you are currently using my mods in a modpack via Modrinth, everything will continue to work normally.
  • Current downloads: Existing versions will remain available for download.
  • No new updates: Even if a project does not appear fully "archived" immediately, absolutely no new versions will be released on Modrinth.

Thank you for understanding and supporting my work. I look forward to seeing you over on CurseForge!
